## 1. Editorial Policy & Safety Criteria

To protect domain authority and prevent Google search demotions or spam penalties:
- **Zero Toxic Links:** Never participate in Private Blog Networks (PBNs), automated comment blasts, link exchanges ("link schemes"), or paid spam directories.
- **Strict Gaming Niche:** Every citation source must be an established gaming directory, indie game aggregator, open-source web tech showcase, or educational unblocked directory.
- **Natural Anchor Profiles:** Prioritize branded anchors (`Pawcade`, `Pawcade Games`, `pawcadegames.com`) and descriptive game anchors (`Play Duck Runner online`, `Farm Mini Mart browser game`).
