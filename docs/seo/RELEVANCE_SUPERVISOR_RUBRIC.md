# SEO Relevance Supervisor — Evaluation Rubric & Audit Log

**Supervisor Agent:** `seo-quality-supervisor`  
**Purpose:** Guarding brand integrity, content relevance, backlink safety, and minimalist user experience.  
**Passing Threshold:** >= 90 / 100 points  

---

## 1. Five-Pillar Evaluation Formula

```
Overall Relevance Score = P1 (Thematic Purity) 
                        + P2 (Search Intent Fit) 
                        + P3 (Human Tone & Clarity) 
                        + P4 (Backlink Safety) 
                        + P5 (UX Stealth & Minimalism)
```

### Pillar Definitions:

1. **Pillar 1: Thematic Purity (0–20 pts)**
   - **20 pts:** 100% focused on casual web games, cute pets, farm animals, and brain puzzles.
   - **10 pts:** General gaming reference with light divergence.
   - **0 pts:** Irrelevant niches (gambling, crypto, get-rich-quick, unrelated software).

2. **Pillar 2: Search Intent Fit (0–20 pts)**
   - **20 pts:** Directly targets authentic US browser-gamer intent (free online, no download, unblocked, casual play).
   - **10 pts:** Overly generic or misleading keywords.
   - **0 pts:** Clickbait queries that don't match actual gameplay.

3. **Pillar 3: Human Tone & Clarity (0–20 pts)**
   - **20 pts:** Written naturally from the perspective of an indie game studio; engaging, clear, and grammatically impeccable.
   - **10 pts:** Slightly repetitive or stiff text.
   - **0 pts:** Robotic AI spam or awkward keyword stuffing.

4. **Pillar 4: Backlink Safety (0–20 pts)**
   - **20 pts:** High-trust, editorial, white-hat gaming domains (itch.io, ProductHunt, HTML5 game directories).
   - **10 pts:** Unknown or unindexed directory.
   - **0 pts:** PBNs, comment spam, link-exchange rings, or toxic domains.

5. **Pillar 5: UX Stealth & "Games-Only" Protection (0–20 pts)**
   - **20 pts:** 100% preserves clean game screen; homepage is pure icons; game pages have games front-and-center with SEO nested in clean, collapsible sections or Schema.org JSON-LD.
   - **10 pts:** Minor text clutter below fold.
   - **0 pts:** Intrusive text blocks on homepage or over game canvas.
