## 2. Outreach Protocol & Code of Conduct

1. **Explicit Permission Required:** No external outreach, emails, or form submissions may be executed without explicit sign-off from the Queen Agent.
2. **Value First:** Every contact pitch must clearly state why our browser game provides value to *their* readers (e.g. 100% free, zero ads, zero downloads, verified solvable puzzles).
3. **Blacklisted Practices:**
   - ❌ No payment for links or sponsorships disguised as editorial.
   - ❌ No link exchanges or reciprocal link rings.
   - ❌ No automated blog/forum comment drops.
