## 2. Topic Clustering Strategy

Instead of creating fragmented, thin doorway pages, Pawcade bundles search demand into **4 core thematic pillars**:

1. **Pillar 1: Spatial Unblocking & Escape Puzzles**
   - *Core Cluster:* Dog Parking, Cat Parking, Animal Farm Parking, Critter Tap Away, Horseshoe Twist.
   - *Dominant Intent:* Logic, spatial untangling, sliding obstacles, unlocking gates.
2. **Pillar 2: Modern Viral Mechanics (App-to-Web Demolition & Sorting)**
   - *Core Cluster:* Farm Block Blast, Sheep Cart Jam, Farm Pantry Sort, Egg Buster, Grain Sand Fall.
   - *Dominant Intent:* Casual instant play of mobile sensations on desktop/Chromebook without downloads.
3. **Pillar 3: Reflex Arcade & Endless Flight/Runners**
   - *Core Cluster:* Duck Runner, Flappy Chicken, Sheep Stampede.
   - *Dominant Intent:* Quick-session study break play, score chasing, tap/steer reflex controls.
4. **Pillar 4: Cozy Farm Tycoons & Merge Progression**
   - *Core Cluster:* Farm Mini Mart, Chef Rat Rush, Animal 2048, Farm Animal Match, Farm Tetris.
   - *Dominant Intent:* Low-stress simulation, customer order fulfillment, tile merging, satisfying progression.
